[Static shot] A painted storybook night scene: a tiny hazel dormouse with ginger fur and a big spiral tail sleeps curled up in a little nest of hazel leaves on a branch. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. A tiny golden seed of light, a small bright glowing point, floats in front of her nose. First, the little seed slowly circles once around her nose while she sleeps, breathing softly. Then her nose twitches, she sniffs, her eyes open wide, and she sits up on her haunches in the nest, her spiral tail curling up behind her. Last, she looks up at the seed, which drifts up just above her head, and she raises both tiny front paws toward it. The seed stays small and bright the whole time. The branch, the nest, the leaves and the night sky stay completely still. Sound: a soft night forest ambience only.
