[Static shot] A painted storybook night scene: a tiny hazel dormouse with ginger fur sleeps curled up in a little nest of hazel leaves on a branch, her big spiral tail curled up behind her back. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. A tiny golden seed of light, a small bright glowing point, floats in the air in front of her face. First, the little seed drifts slowly in a small loop in the air around the tip of her nose, always in front of her face and above the nest, while she sleeps and breathes softly. Then her nose twitches, she sniffs, her eyes open wide, and she sits up on her haunches in the same spot, her tail staying curled up behind her back. Last, she looks up at the seed, which floats up just above her head, and she raises both tiny front paws toward it. The seed stays small and bright the whole time. The branch, the nest, the leaves and the night sky stay completely still. Sound: a soft night forest ambience only.
