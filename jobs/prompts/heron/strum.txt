[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat plays his small ukulele. The whole time, the wing-tip resting over the sound hole strums in an even, steady rhythm: it sweeps clearly down across all four strings, then back up across them, down and up again, a relaxed movement from the wrist, the same size every stroke. The other wing-tip stays still on the neck. His head bobs very slightly with each down stroke, beak closed, eye calm and open. He ends exactly in his starting pose. His hat, his straw cape, his body, the ukulele, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
