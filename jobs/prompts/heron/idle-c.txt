[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat holds his small ukulele against his chest. First the wing-tip resting over the sound hole lets go of the strings, rises slowly to the front brim of his straw hat, touches it lightly to straighten the hat, and goes back down to rest over the sound hole. Then he slowly shifts his weight, settling his body a little lower on his standing leg and rising back up, resting in the starting pose between the two movements. Beak closed the whole time. He ends exactly in his starting pose. The ukulele, the wing-tip on its neck, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
