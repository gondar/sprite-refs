[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat holds his small ukulele still against his chest. First he slowly tilts his head to one side, as if listening to something far away, holds it for a moment, and brings it back upright. Then he slowly raises his head and long beak to look up at the sky, a calm, curious look, and lowers it again, resting in the starting pose between the two movements. Beak closed the whole time. He ends exactly in his starting pose. The ukulele, both wing-tips, his straw cape, his body, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
