[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat plays one single down strum on his small ukulele. Only the grey wing-tip resting over the sound hole moves: it sweeps smoothly down and to the right across all four strings and comes to rest just below the sound hole, feather tips pointing down. The ukulele stays in exactly the same place and angle the whole time. The other wing-tip stays still on the neck. His head, beak and eye stay still, beak closed. His hat, his straw cape, his body, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
