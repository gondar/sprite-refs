[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat holds his small ukulele still against his chest. First he gives his body a small shake, so that the layers of his straw cape lift slightly and ripple and settle back down, the way a bird ruffles its feathers. Then he closes his eye in one slow, calm blink and gives a small, slow nod of the head, resting in the starting pose between the two movements. Beak closed the whole time. He ends exactly in his starting pose. The ukulele, both wing-tips, his hat, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
