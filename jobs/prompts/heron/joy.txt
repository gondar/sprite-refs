[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A tall, dignified grey heron musician in a straw rain cape and a wide straw hat holds his small ukulele against his chest. First he plays one big, proud flourish: the wing-tip over the sound hole sweeps down across all the strings and opens out to the side in an elegant arc, then comes back to rest over the sound hole. Then he makes a slow, dignified bow, lowering his head and long beak toward the audience on the right, and rises again, resting in the starting pose between the two movements. Beak closed the whole time. He ends exactly in his starting pose. The ukulele, the wing-tip on its neck, his leg, his foot and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet riverside ambience only.
