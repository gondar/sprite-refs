[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A small friendly cartoon green alien in a silver space suit stands facing us. First he plays an imaginary ukulele held in front of his belly: his left hand holds the invisible neck and his right hand strums happily up and down, his head bobbing to the beat. Then he lowers his hands and bows politely to us, and straightens up again. He ends exactly in his starting pose, lips pressed together in a small closed-mouth smile, his big eyes calm. The plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet low hum only.
