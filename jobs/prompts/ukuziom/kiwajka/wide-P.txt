[Static shot] The camera is locked, the framing never changes and the lighting never changes. Nothing appears behind her. A small cheerful cartoon monkey stands holding a small light-wood ukulele across her belly in playing position. She strums the ukulele happily: her strumming hand brushes down and up across the strings in a steady rhythm, eight quick strums, while her head bobs gently to the beat and her tail sways, her other hand keeping its fingers on the neck. Then she stops and holds still. She ends exactly in her starting pose, holding the ukulele the same way. The ukulele keeps exactly the same shape, size and colour the whole time. Her feet stay planted. She keeps the same happy open smile and never talks. The plain empty backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft, happy ukulele strumming only.
