[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A small friendly cartoon green alien in a silver space suit stands facing us. He jogs happily in place: small bouncy running steps on the spot, his knees lifting one after the other, his arms swinging in rhythm, his two antennae with yellow balls bobbing with every step. He keeps the same even pace the whole time and never moves forward or sideways. At the very end he slows down. He ends exactly in his starting pose, lips pressed together in a small closed-mouth smile, his big eyes calm. The plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet low hum only.
