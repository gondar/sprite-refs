[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A painted cardboard theatre puppet of a townsman in a fur hat and a long fur-trimmed coat stands side-on, facing right. First he leans forward and shoves an invisible heavy object in front of him with both hands, twice, his boots sliding, his face strained with his lips pressed together. Then he jumps back startled with both arms flung up, as if something just kicked at him, and settles. He ends exactly in his starting pose. His face, hat, coat and painted style stay exactly the same, and the plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft winter wind only.
