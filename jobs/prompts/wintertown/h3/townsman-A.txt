[Static shot] The camera is locked and the lighting never changes. A painted cardboard theatre puppet of a townsman in a fur hat and a long fur-trimmed coat stands side-on, facing right. First he grabs an invisible rope in front of him with both hands, leans far back and hauls on it twice with all his strength, heels dug in. Then the rope slips: he falls backwards and sits down hard on his bottom, legs in the air for a moment, then gets back up and brushes off his coat. He ends exactly in his starting pose, lips pressed together. His face, hat, coat and painted style stay exactly the same, and the plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft winter wind only.
