[Static shot] A small painted storybook hazel dormouse with ginger fur and a big spiral tail sits upright on its haunches in side view, facing right. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the even, flat lighting never change. It makes two gentle movements, resting in its starting pose between them. First, it raises both front paws to its face and washes it, rubbing its paws down over its cheeks and nose a few times, eyes half closed. Then it lowers its paws, holds them together at its chest, and lifts its head to gaze upward in quiet wonder for a moment, before bringing its head back level. It ends exactly in its starting pose, mouth closed in a small smile. Its tail, hind feet and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft garden ambience only.
