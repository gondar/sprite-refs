[Static shot] A small painted storybook hazel dormouse with ginger fur and a big spiral tail sits upright on its haunches in side view, facing right. The camera stays locked off; the framing and lighting never change. It watches something tiny fluttering over it. First, its round ears perk up and it looks ahead and a little down, then its head and big eyes follow the tiny thing upward in one smooth, steady arc until it gazes straight up above its own head, chin tipped high. Then its gaze follows it back down along the same arc to the front. It stays in side view the whole time. It ends exactly in its starting pose, mouth closed in a small smile. Its body, front paws, tail, hind feet and the plain backdrop stay completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet ambience only.
