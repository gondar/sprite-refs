Then it climbs back down the same short way, paw under paw, gripping the twig firmly, until it is back where it started. It ends exactly in its starting pose and place, mouth closed. The twig itself never moves, bends or changes, and the plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft garden ambience only.
