[Static shot] an all-white heraldic cartoon eagle with a golden beak, golden talons, a golden band ending in a trefoil on each wing, a scaled white breast and a white fleur-de-lis tail with a golden ring, flies to the right in side view, wings raised. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. The eagle stays in the same place in the frame; its body does not travel. It climbs with big, slow, mighty wingbeats, one after another, all the same: each time it throws both wings far down until the wingtips sweep below its talons, and its whole body surges upward with the stroke, then it sinks back a little while its wings rise high and open wide above its back again. Its head stays level and looks ahead to the right, eye fierce and bright. Its talons stay tucked, its tail stays straight. It stays all white and gold. It ends exactly in its starting pose.
