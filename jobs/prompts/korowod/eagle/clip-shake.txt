[Static shot] A young white cartoon eagle with a golden beak, golden talons and a golden band on each wing flies to the right in side view, wings raised. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. The eagle stays in the same place in the frame; its body does not travel. It makes two movements, flying calmly between them. First it gives a quick happy shake of its whole body, ruffling all its feathers so they puff out, then they settle smooth again. Then it lifts its head and gives a short joyful screech, beak wide open for a moment, then closes its beak. Its wings keep beating gently. It ends exactly in its starting pose. Sound: soft wind and one short eagle cry.
