[Static shot] A young white cartoon eagle with a golden beak, golden talons and a golden band on each wing flies to the right in side view, wings raised. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. The eagle stays in the same place in the frame; its body does not travel. Then it does a proud swoop: it dips its head and body down a little and swings back up with a strong wingbeat. Beak closed, eye bright and cheerful. It ends exactly in its starting pose.
