Drawn in the style of a 1970s Polish comic book (the Papcio Chmiel school, Tytus Romek i A'Tomek era) — exactly the style of the reference image: bold lively black brush-ink outlines of varying weight, flat bright offset-print colours, simple ink hatching for shade, cheerful cartoon proportions with big heads, round noses, big expressive friendly faces. Every figure is a paper cut-out sticker: a clean white scissor-cut card border about 12 px wide runs all around its black ink outline. Drawn flat-on, each figure stands on its own feet: no ground, no grass, no cast shadow, no scenery. Do not draw any existing comic character, only the style.
