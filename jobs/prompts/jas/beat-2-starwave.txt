[Static shot] The camera is locked and does not move; the lighting never changes. A cheerful boy carol singer with a red hat and a red cape holds a tall pole with a glowing paper star. He stands in place and waves the star pole left and right in time to a carol, a steady, even beat: the pole tilts a little to one side, then a little to the other, like a metronome, six even swings, all the same size and speed. The pole stays firmly in his left hand, which rocks gently at the wrist; the paper star at the top stays whole as it sways. His feet stay planted and his body stays upright; his head sways very slightly with the beat. He ends exactly in his starting pose, facing the viewer, holding the pole upright. His lips stay pressed together in a small smile the whole time. The plain backdrop stays completely still. Sound: a soft, quiet winter street ambience only.
