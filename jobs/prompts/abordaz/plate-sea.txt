A wide panoramic watercolour painting of an open sunny sea, for a children's game background. Use the attached moodboard's LEFT page painting as the quality bar for the sky and water; use its colours.

Composition (wide, 21:9): the horizon is a calm straight line at 40% of the height from the top. Above it: a soft blue summer sky painted wet-in-wet, a few soft cumulus clouds with lavender-grey shadows (#A9B3CC), a gentle warm sun glow in the upper LEFT corner. Below it: open sea in transparent glazes — paler turquoise-grey far away near the horizon (atmospheric perspective), deepening to rich blue-teal in the foreground, with gentle rolling swell, small lifted-white wave crests, and sparkling sun glints on the water under the sun. The bottom 40% of the picture is calmer, simpler water (it will sit behind a reading panel), with only soft horizontal wave strokes.

EMPTY scene: no ship, no boat, no island, no land, no birds, no people, no animals, no objects, no frame, no border, no paper edges, no text, no letters, no signature. Just sky and sea, painted with care: craft, not objects.

Style: a European watercolour picture-book illustration. Wet-in-wet sky, transparent glazes, sun glints and foam lifted out of the white paper, visible soft paper grain. Characters are simple, friendly figures with a thin, even sepia outline (#3A3330) and flat watercolour fills, big readable silhouettes. Sunny summer palette, warm but mid-key, NOT high contrast, not busy: sky #7DB4E0, sea #3C9BA8 to deep #2F6F8F, ship wood #A8744A, sails #EAD9B5, pirate red #D2603F, navy #33476B, treasure gold #E2B04A.
