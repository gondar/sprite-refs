[Static shot] A painted storybook scene at the first light of dawn: a tiny hazel dormouse with ginger fur and a big spiral tail glides down through the air toward her little nest of hazel leaves on a branch. The camera stays locked off; the framing and the lighting never change. First, she sails down in a gentle arc and lands softly in the nest on all four paws, with a small squash, and settles. Then she turns around once in the nest, tucks her front paws under her chin and curls up into a soft round ball, her big spiral tail curled up behind her back like a blanket. Last, her eyes slowly close, she gives a small contented sigh and lies still, asleep. The branch, the nest, the leaves and the sky stay completely still. Sound: a soft dawn forest ambience only.
