The hazel dormouse (orzesznica) looks exactly like the one in the reference: golden-ginger body, cream-white throat and belly, big glossy black eyes, small rounded ears. Its tail is long, slender and evenly ginger, covered in soft fur along its whole length, with no stripes or rings; the tail hangs down or wraps gently, never curls upward like a squirrel's. The body is slim and mouse-like, not round like a hamster.
