SCENE: The Coronation of Mary, high in a vast dawn sky. LOW KEY.

PALETTE AND LIGHT (most important): a dim, deep golden dawn with strong contrast, like an old gouache painting. The upper sky is a deep cool blue-grey and dusky slate (#56687a to #6f8396), darkening towards the top edge; large spiral cloud shapes in the sky are cool grey with dark shadowed undersides. ONE warm light source: the low golden dawn glow at the horizon on the lower right, which lights only the tops of the clouds and Mary's face and mantle with warm gold. Deep shadows: dark blue-grey cloud undersides, dark blue misty hills of the earth far below. Not pastel, not cream. STRONG CONTRAST between light and dark: a luminous band of glowing golden-apricot dawn light (pale gold near the horizon, the brightest part of the picture) runs behind the cloud bank and behind Mary on the right, and fades upward into warm dusky gold-ochre around and above Mary's head, then into deep slate blue-grey at the top. The cloud tops facing the dawn are bright warm gold with peach highlights; their undersides and the far hills are deep dark blue-grey. Like the golden dawn of Image 1, but deeper and more dramatic.

Mary sits on the right end of a long cloud, slightly right of centre: the same young village girl, natural face, no blush, small dark eyes, dark brown hair under a rough beige head cloth, a plain faded blue mantle over a grey-blue homespun dress, barefoot, hands folded loosely in her lap, a quiet, surprised small smile, looking slightly down. A faint warm light falls on her from above.

Above her head there is EMPTY, CLEAR, warm dusky-gold sky: a calm open space about two head-heights tall directly above her head, with nothing painted in it: no stars, no ring, no halo, no crown, no rays, no cloud. (A crown of light will be added later.)

Across the lower-left and centre of the frame lies a long, THICK cloud bank with a wide FLAT top drawn as a clear pencil line, its top lit warm gold by the dawn and its underside in cool grey shadow; it is plain, with Celtic spiral curls only at its edges; the left part of its flat top is empty and calm. Plain flat gouache clouds only: no flowers, no bushes, no plants on any cloud.

Far below, along the bottom edge, the small dark blue misty hills of the earth; the lower-left band of hills is plain and calm.

Composition: candid and asymmetric, a lot of quiet deep sky; no throne, no angels, no other figures.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, textured gouache fills with paper grain, slightly flattened perspective. Spirals live only in nature and light (bushes, clouds, wind, rays of light), never as ornament on clothes or buildings. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: take the palette of Image 1 but in a LOWER, DARKER key: deep cool blue-grey and slate dominate, one warm golden dawn glow low on the horizon. Gentle, quiet, not saturated, not candy. Holiness is shown only by soft white light: a soft glow, fine spiral rays. Nothing metallic, no gold objects, no gold halos.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: the people are poor and simple: coarse undyed wool, faded patched homespun, rough head cloths, bare dusty feet, working hands. Nothing ornamental, no jewellery, no crowns of metal, no patterned borders, no thrones, no pomp.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, slightly off-centre, as if we caught a passing moment; the figures do not look at the viewer and are not posed. Wide 16:9, full bleed, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. Each figure is a readable silhouette with a little space of background between figures. Only the people described appear; no animals, no birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
