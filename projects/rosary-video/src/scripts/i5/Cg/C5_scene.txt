SCENE: The Coronation of Mary, in a vast golden-dawn sky.

A wide open dawn sky: peach and apricot glow low on the horizon, rising into cool blue-grey at the top, soft spiral-shaped clouds drifting, fine white rays of light. Far below at the bottom edge, the tiny rounded blue-grey hills of the earth in morning mist.

Mary is seated on a soft, simple white cloud, slightly right of centre, small in this big sky: a humble young woman with a gentle face, in a plain faded blue mantle over a simple grey homespun dress, a plain undyed head cloth under the mantle, barefoot, her hands folded loosely on her lap, smiling quietly and looking down a little, modest and surprised. Above her head floats a light circlet of twelve small white stars of LIGHT, just glowing points of light arranged in a ring, not metal, nothing gold, no crown object. A soft white glow behind her.

On the lower left, a long low flat ledge of soft dawn-lit cloud, like a gentle shelf of cloud stretching across the lower third of the frame and empty, calm and free of figures, with its top edge drawn as a clear soft line.

Composition: candid and asymmetric, much quiet sky; nothing ornamental, no throne, no angels, no other figures.
