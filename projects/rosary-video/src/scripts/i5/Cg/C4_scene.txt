SCENE: The Assumption of Mary, dawn over a rocky garden tomb.

On the left, a simple tomb cut into a grey rock hillside, its rough stone opening wide open; the tomb is EMPTY of any body and instead it is FULL of fresh roses, white and soft pink, spilling over its stone edge onto the ground. A plain linen burial cloth lies folded among the roses.

Above the tomb, a little to the right, Mary is gently rising in soft white light: an older but simple woman with a kind face, in a plain faded blue-grey homespun dress and a rough undyed head cloth, barefoot, her hands slightly open at her sides, smiling quietly, her eyes looking upward. Around her a soft oval glow of white light with fine spiral rays, peach-apricot dawn sky behind. She floats calmly, no wings, no angels, no crown.

Below, on the ground to the right of the tomb, two apostles in rough brown and grey patched homespun, poor and simple, separated by a little gap: an old bearded man has just come to the tomb and stops with both hands lifted in amazement, looking up; a younger one kneels by the roses, holding a rose, looking up at her with open mouth and a glad face.

The place: a rocky garden of grey stone with olive trees with spiral trunks, spiral-leaved bushes, small wild flowers, a dusty path. The sun is rising behind low cool blue-grey hills on the right.

Composition: candid and asymmetric. The lower-right and lower-centre foreground is an open stretch of dusty path with a flat grey stone, calm and free of figures.
