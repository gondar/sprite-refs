SCENE: The Ascension of Jesus, on a grassy hilltop at dawn.

The top of the frame: a big soft luminous white-and-peach cloud, glowing from inside, with fine spiral rays of white light. Of Jesus we see ONLY the hem of a plain white robe and two bare feet, just disappearing up into the cloud at the top centre-left of the picture; the rest of him is hidden inside the cloud.

Below, on the rounded hilltop, four apostles, simple poor fishermen in rough brown, grey and faded blue homespun with patches, bare or sandalled dusty feet, stand in a loose uneven group, each a separate silhouette with a little gap between them, all looking up at the cloud. One young apostle shades his eyes with his hand against the light; an older bearded one has his mouth open in wonder and holds his staff; one has dropped to one knee in the grass; one has a hand half raised, as if he would wave goodbye. Faces amazed and gently happy, not dramatic.

The place: a wide rounded hilltop of soft sage-green grass with small white and pale wild flowers, a few grey stones, a worn footpath; one small spiral-leaved bush. Behind and below, distant cool blue-grey hills and the faint walls of a small town in the valley, morning mist. The sun's peach-apricot glow comes from the cloud and the low sky behind it.

Composition: the apostles stand on the right half of the hilltop, in the lower-middle of the frame; the cloud fills the upper centre-left. The lower-left foreground is an open, empty grassy slope with a flat grey stone, calm and free of figures.

Image 1 is the MAIN STYLE REFERENCE: match its hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache texture, painterly paper grain, confident pencil outlines, its golden-dawn palette (peach and apricot sky glow over cool blue-grey hills and shadows, soft sage greens) and its white radiant light drawn as fine rays and Celtic spirals; small simple wild flowers and spiral-leaved bushes in nature. Image 2 is a reference ONLY for how poor and simple the people are and how the figures are drawn (angular Kells-like figures, plain patched homespun clothes, bare feet). Do not copy the content of either image.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, textured gouache fills with paper grain, slightly flattened perspective. Spirals live only in nature and light (bushes, clouds, wind, rays of light), never as ornament on clothes or buildings. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: golden dawn on cool blue-grey, as in Image 1: a warm peach-apricot glow from one light source, the rest of the scene in soft cool blue-grey and muted sage shadows. Gentle, quiet, not saturated, not candy. Holiness is shown only by soft white light: a soft glow, fine spiral rays. Nothing metallic, no gold objects, no gold halos.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: the people are poor and simple: coarse undyed wool, faded patched homespun, rough head cloths, bare dusty feet, working hands. Nothing ornamental, no jewellery, no crowns of metal, no patterned borders, no thrones, no pomp.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, slightly off-centre, as if we caught a passing moment; the figures do not look at the viewer and are not posed. Wide 16:9, full bleed, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. Each figure is a readable silhouette with a little space of background between figures. Only the people described appear; no animals, no birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
