SCENE: The Ascension of Jesus, on a grassy hilltop at dawn.

The top of the frame: a big soft luminous white-and-peach cloud, glowing from inside, with fine spiral rays of white light. Of Jesus we see ONLY the hem of a plain white robe and two bare feet, just disappearing up into the cloud at the top centre-left of the picture; the rest of him is hidden inside the cloud.

Below, on the rounded hilltop, four apostles, simple poor fishermen in rough brown, grey and faded blue homespun with patches, bare or sandalled dusty feet, stand in a loose uneven group, each a separate silhouette with a little gap between them, all looking up at the cloud. One young apostle shades his eyes with his hand against the light; an older bearded one has his mouth open in wonder and holds his staff; one has dropped to one knee in the grass; one has a hand half raised, as if he would wave goodbye. Faces amazed and gently happy, not dramatic.

The place: a wide rounded hilltop of soft sage-green grass with small white and pale wild flowers, a few grey stones, a worn footpath; one small spiral-leaved bush. Behind and below, distant cool blue-grey hills and the faint walls of a small town in the valley, morning mist. The sun's peach-apricot glow comes from the cloud and the low sky behind it.

Composition: the apostles stand on the right half of the hilltop, in the lower-middle of the frame; the cloud fills the upper centre-left. The lower-left foreground is an open, empty grassy slope with a flat grey stone, calm and free of figures.
