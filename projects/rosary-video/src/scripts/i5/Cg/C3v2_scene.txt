SCENE: Pentecost, the coming of the Holy Spirit, in a poor upper room at dawn.

A small poor room of rough mud-plaster walls with soft rounded corners, a packed beaten-earth floor, two low rough-hewn dark beams only on the right part of the ceiling. On the right a small square window opening; a plain undyed linen cloth hangs from a single nail over it and blows hard into the room; through the window the warm peach-apricot glow of dawn. Golden dawn light rakes across the earth floor from the window. A rushing wind drawn as ONE soft white spiral ribbon sweeps from the window through the room, lifting two loose blank sheets of parchment into the air. A clay water jar stands by the right wall, a worn reed mat lies on the floor.

The figures stand and sit in a loose arc across the middle of the room, each one fully separate with clear bare floor between them:
- far left: an old bearded apostle in a patched brown robe, barefoot, his arms half opened in astonishment;
- left of centre: Mary sits on the LEFT part of a long low rough wooden bench, hands open on her lap, looking up with a quiet joyful smile; the RIGHT half of the bench beside her is empty bare wood, a clear empty space as wide as her body;
- right of centre: a young apostle stands laughing with joy, both hands holding his head cloth against the wind;
- further right, a step away from the young one with a gap of bare earth floor between them: a bearded apostle kneels on the reed mat, looking up with wide eyes; his whole body is clear of the young one's robe.
Above EACH head, clearly above its own head, floats one small soft tongue of flame made of warm light, its glow curling into tiny Celtic spiral curls of light; each flame lights its own face from above.

Light: low key. The small flames and the dawn window are the only light; the room corners and the ceiling are in cool blue-grey shadow, the walls warm ochre where the light touches them.

Composition: candid, asymmetric. The UPPER-LEFT part of the wall above the old apostle is plain, calm and empty: no beam, no flame, no window, just quiet shadowed plaster. The lower foreground is an open band of bare earth floor.
