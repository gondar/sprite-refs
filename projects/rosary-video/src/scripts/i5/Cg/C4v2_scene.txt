SCENE: The Assumption of Mary, dawn over a rocky garden tomb.

On the left, a simple tomb cut into a grey rock hillside with a rough stone trough, its stone lid pushed aside; the tomb is EMPTY of any body and instead FULL of fresh roses, white and soft pink, spilling over its stone edge onto the dusty ground. A plain folded linen burial cloth lies among the roses.

Above the tomb, a little right of it, Mary is gently rising: the same young village girl, smooth young face, dark brown hair under a rough beige head cloth, plain grey-blue patched homespun dress, barefoot, hands slightly open at her sides, a quiet happy smile, eyes looking upward. Holiness only as a SOFT ROUND glow of white light around her, fading gently into the sky, with faint thin spiral rays; no hard outline, no oval mandorla, no wings, no angels, no crown.

Below on the right, two apostles in rough brown and grey patched homespun, barefoot, poor and simple, standing APART with a clear gap of dusty ground between them, no overlap at all: an old bearded man stands in the middle-right, stopped with both hands lifted in amazement, looking up; further right and a little closer to the viewer, a young man kneels on the ground holding a single rose, looking up at her with an open, glad face.

The place: a rocky garden of grey stone, olive trees with twisting Celtic-spiral trunks, spiral-leaved bushes, small wild flowers, a dusty path. The low sun rises behind cool blue-grey hills on the right. The upper-right sky above the sun is plain and calm.

Composition: candid and asymmetric. The lower-centre foreground is an open stretch of dusty path with a flat grey stone, calm and free of figures.
