SCENE: The Coronation of Mary, high in a vast dawn sky. LOW KEY.

PALETTE AND LIGHT (most important): a dim, deep golden dawn with strong contrast, like an old gouache painting. The upper sky is a deep cool blue-grey and dusky slate (#56687a to #6f8396), darkening towards the top edge; large spiral cloud shapes in the sky are cool grey with dark shadowed undersides. ONE warm light source: the low golden dawn glow at the horizon on the lower right, which lights only the tops of the clouds and Mary's face and mantle with warm gold. Deep shadows: dark blue-grey cloud undersides, dark blue misty hills of the earth far below. Not pastel, not cream, not bright; no white-out areas.

Mary sits on the right end of a long cloud, slightly right of centre: the same young village girl, natural face, no blush, small dark eyes, dark brown hair under a rough beige head cloth, a plain faded blue mantle over a grey-blue homespun dress, barefoot, hands folded loosely in her lap, a quiet, surprised small smile, looking slightly down. A faint warm light falls on her from above.

Above her head there is EMPTY, CLEAR, dark-gold dusky sky: a calm open space about two head-heights tall directly above her head, with nothing painted in it: no stars, no ring, no halo, no crown, no rays, no cloud. (A crown of light will be added later.)

Across the lower-left and centre of the frame lies a long, THICK cloud bank with a wide FLAT top drawn as a clear pencil line, its top lit warm gold by the dawn and its underside in cool grey shadow; it is plain, with Celtic spiral curls only at its edges; the left part of its flat top is empty and calm. Plain flat gouache clouds only: no flowers, no bushes, no plants on any cloud.

Far below, along the bottom edge, the small dark blue misty hills of the earth; the lower-left band of hills is plain and calm.

Composition: candid and asymmetric, a lot of quiet deep sky; no throne, no angels, no other figures.
