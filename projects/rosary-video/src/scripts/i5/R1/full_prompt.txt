SCENE: The Annunciation as a tender, intimate moment in a walled spring garden in the early morning. Palette: pale mint green, cream, blush rose, soft pale gold light, white flowers everywhere; fresh, airy and luminous.

Left of centre, a tall slender angel made of soft light, cream and pale-gold robe drawn as one long triangular shape, wings made of curling Celtic spirals of light, LEANS DOWN in a graceful curve and WHISPERS INTO MARY'S EAR, one hand cupped beside his mouth as if telling a happy secret, the other hand holding a single tall white lily. His almond eyes are smiling.
Right of centre, young Mary, a girl of about fifteen in a blush-rose dress and a pale mint veil, SITS on a low stone garden wall with an open book on her lap, one hand still on the page; her head is tilted toward the angel, her almond eyes wide, a small surprised smile on her tiny mouth, as if she has just heard something wonderful.
The two figures lean toward each other but do not touch; a narrow sliver of background shows between the angel's cupped hand and Mary's head.

Behind them, a large round stone window/arch opening in an old garden wall frames the pair like a circle, through which pale morning sky and a soft golden sun glow; spiral rose bushes and white flowers climb the wall; spiral-leafed trees on both sides. The figures sit in the upper-middle part of the picture; in front of the low wall the open winding garden path crosses the lower third.
Image 1 and Image 2 are STYLE REFERENCES only: match their hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache texture, Celtic spiral nature, glowing round light and painterly paper grain. Do not copy their content, palette or night mood.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers, pushed harder toward bold graphic design: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, confident dark pencil outlines, textured gouache fills with paper grain. Nature is stylised into Celtic spirals: trees, clouds, wind and light rays drawn as decorative swirls. Flattened, slightly isometric perspective like an illuminated manuscript. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: robes drawn as simple triangular shapes, long necks, small round heads, almond eyes, tiny mouths, long slender hands. Holiness is shown only with light and pattern, a soft circle of light, never gold plates.
MOOD: intimate, human, tender and a little playful, a captured moment in the middle of a gesture, like a frame from an animated film. Not solemn, not a church painting, no frozen ceremonial poses, not realistic, not 3D.

COMPOSITION RULES: wide 16:9, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. The figures stand clearly apart against the background, each figure a separate readable silhouette with a little background visible between separate figures. In the lower third a winding ground path crosses the foreground from left to right; its surface stays open and empty, with pebbles, grass tufts and flowers only along its edges. Only the people described appear; no animals, no birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.

EXTRA STYLE PUSH: draw the figures in the angular graphic manner of The Secret of Kells, not in a rounded cute storybook manner: elongated bodies, robes as bold triangles and long tapering shapes, small heads on long necks, narrow almond-shaped eyes with tiny dot pupils, tiny mouths, pointed slender fingers. Use bolder, flatter colour areas and stronger contrast between figures and background.
