SCENE: The Annunciation as a tender, intimate moment in a walled spring garden in the early morning. Palette: pale mint green, cream, blush rose, soft pale gold light, white flowers everywhere; fresh, airy and luminous.

Left of centre, a tall slender angel made of soft light, cream and pale-gold robe drawn as one long triangular shape, wings made of curling Celtic spirals of light, LEANS DOWN in a graceful curve and WHISPERS INTO MARY'S EAR, one hand cupped beside his mouth as if telling a happy secret, the other hand holding a single tall white lily. His almond eyes are smiling.
Right of centre, young Mary, a girl of about fifteen in a blush-rose dress and a pale mint veil, SITS on a low stone garden wall with an open book on her lap, one hand still on the page; her head is tilted toward the angel, her almond eyes wide, a small surprised smile on her tiny mouth, as if she has just heard something wonderful.
The two figures lean toward each other but do not touch; a narrow sliver of background shows between the angel's cupped hand and Mary's head.

Behind them, a large round stone window/arch opening in an old garden wall frames the pair like a circle, through which pale morning sky and a soft golden sun glow; spiral rose bushes and white flowers climb the wall; spiral-leafed trees on both sides. The figures sit in the upper-middle part of the picture; in front of the low wall the open winding garden path crosses the lower third.
