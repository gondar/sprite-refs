Image 1, Image 2 and Image 3 are STYLE REFERENCES only. Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its earthy ochre, khaki, olive, clay and sand palette, its grounded sober mood, and the simple natural way it draws people. Image 2 shows how a poor interior is lit in the evening by one small oil lamp: take its low key, deep shadows and single warm pool of light. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the subtle Celtic spirals in light and smoke. Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the blue night palette of Image 3.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1.

SCENE: the Last Supper as a real, poor evening meal. A bare upper room with cracked ochre mud-plaster walls, two rough dark wooden ceiling beams and a packed earth floor covered by worn reed mats. In the middle of the room a long, very low rough plank table stands right on the mats, only knee-high. On it: a few flat round loaves, a chipped clay jug, a few plain clay bowls, and one small clay oil lamp with a tiny flame. Everyone sits on the floor around this low table, the way people really ate.

EXACTLY THIRTEEN PEOPLE, placed like this:
- Jesus: an ordinary working man of about thirty, dark hair and short beard, plain worn undyed beige tunic, sitting cross-legged on the FAR side of the table, slightly right of centre, facing us. Mid-gesture, he breaks a flat round loaf of bread in his two hands above the table, eyes lowered to the bread, quiet and tender. Right beside his hand on the table stands one plain undecorated clay cup of wine. The lamp light falls on his hands, the bread and the cup. No halo.
- Behind the table next to Jesus, 2 apostles: on his left side the beardless young John leaning a little toward him, watching the bread; on his right side a bearded man watching.
- On the LEFT end of the table, 3 apostles sitting on the mats, seen in profile and three-quarter view, turned toward Jesus: one leaning on an elbow, one reaching for a bowl, one older bald man listening.
- On the RIGHT end of the table, 3 apostles sitting on the mats, seen in profile, turned toward Jesus: an older grey-bearded man (Peter) leaning forward, and two others, one with a hand on his knee.
- In the FOREGROUND, on the near side of the table, 4 apostles sitting with their BACKS to the viewer: we see the backs of their heads and shoulders and their rough patched tunics, two of them slightly turned so a cheek shows. They sit in a loose uneven row with gaps between them so that the bread, the cup and Jesus stay clearly visible between their heads.
That is Jesus plus 12 apostles, thirteen people in total and no one else in the room. All in patched undyed homespun clothes in beige, brown, grey and olive, bare feet. Small gaps between the groups. Candid and asymmetric, nobody poses, nobody looks at the viewer; one apostle mid-chew, another passing a bowl.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: evening, LOW KEY. The single warm light source is the oil lamp on the table, making one pool of amber light over the bread, the cup and the faces near Jesus; the faces further away fade into warm umber and olive shadow; the walls are dark ochre. Earthy ochre, olive, clay and sand like Image 1, but dim. No gold, no glare.

COMPOSITION: wide shot from slightly above eye level so the top of the low table, the bread and the cup are visible over the foreground heads. The group fills the middle band of the frame. The bottom-left corner and the bottom edge below the foreground apostles are an open stretch of packed earth floor and reed mat, empty. The upper part is plain dark plaster wall and beams.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched clothes, rough wood, chipped clay. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders. Holiness is shown only by the soft lamp light on the bread.

Hard exclusions: exactly 13 people, no extra people, no children, no servants, no figures in doorways; no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, no halos, no gold chalice, no metal, no tablecloth, no sparkles, no animals, full-bleed image.
