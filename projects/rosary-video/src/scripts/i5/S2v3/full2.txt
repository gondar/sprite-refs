Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its harsh-noon earthy palette (dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached hazy sky), its grounded sober mood, its deep short shadows and the simple natural way it draws people. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the Celtic spirals hidden in nature (vine tendrils, the curl of the wine, wisps of cloud). Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the dim interior palette of Images 2 and 3. Image 4 is an IDENTITY REFERENCE for Mary: the same girl, same face, same beige rough head cloth and same grey-blue patched dress.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Muted, dusty colours, rather dark overall; nothing pastel, nothing candy. Spirals appear only subtly in nature, never as ornament. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1; no rosy cheeks, no big round cartoon eyes.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched undyed homespun clothes, bare feet, rough wood, chipped clay, frayed rope. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders on clothes or pots. Holiness is shown only through light, never by halos.

Create a new wide 16:9 illustration: the wedding at Cana, a poor village courtyard wedding at harsh midday, caught at the moment the water turns into wine.

SETTING: a small dusty courtyard of a poor mud-brick house with cracked ochre plaster walls. Harsh noon sun from high above casts short, deep blue-brown shadows; a ragged olive-green vine on a crooked pole pergola at the right throws dappled dark shade. Upper left: a plain stretch of cracked mud-brick wall and a strip of pale hazy sky above it, calm and empty, with no objects, no lamps, no vines, no figures. On a low ledge of the wall in the shade at the right, two small plain brown clay oil lamps with tiny flames. Far back in the shade of the pergola, three or four small simple guests in plain dusty clothes sit at a low rough table with flat bread and a clay bowl, tiny and unimportant, one of them clapping softly.

FOREGROUND, right of centre: six plain opaque grey-brown stone water jars, rough hand-cut stone like big mortars, of slightly different heights, standing in an irregular row on the dirt, chipped and stained, with no decoration at all; they are not glass and you cannot see through them. The camera looks slightly down, so their open round mouths are visible from above. The front jar, nearest to the viewer at the lower right, is a wide squat stone jar with a broad flat lip. In the open mouth of the jar just left of the front jar, the water surface is turning into deep dark red wine in a single slow spiral swirl, seen from above inside the round mouth only.

FIGURES (candid, mid-moment, not posing; a clear gap of bare ground between each figure, they do not touch):
- Mary, the girl from Image 4, about fourteen or fifteen: beige rough head cloth, plain grey-blue patched dress with rolled sleeves, barefoot, working hands. Left of centre, she leans toward Jesus from a small step away and whispers to him, one hand cupped beside her mouth, her face earnest and a little worried.
- Jesus: an ordinary young working man of about thirty, dark hair and short beard, plain worn undyed homespun tunic, barefoot, standing a little right of Mary with a hand-width gap between them. He turns his head toward her, amused, smiling with his eyes, and holds one hand low and open, palm up, toward the jars. No halo, no glow.
- A barefoot village boy of about seven in a short rough patched tunic stands on tiptoe, heels lifted high off the ground, both hands gripping the rim of the wine jar, which reaches his chest; he stretches his neck to peek down into it with wide amazed eyes and an open mouth.
Figures are of moderate size, adults standing about 55 percent of the frame height.

COMPOSITION: wide shot, slightly asymmetric. Mary and Jesus left of centre to centre, the boy and jars on the right. The bottom fifth is open bare packed earth with a few pebbles and a dropped wooden cup, empty and calm at the lower centre and in front of the front jar. The top-left area (left half, top third) stays calm plain wall and sky. The only living beings are these people.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, no halos, no gold, no lanterns, no coloured lights, no garlands, no flowers, no ornaments or patterns, no animals, full-bleed image.
