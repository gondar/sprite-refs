SCENE: The Presentation in the Temple: a poor young couple brings their forty-day-old baby to the Temple in Jerusalem, in the cold early morning just after sunrise. The whole courtyard is still in deep cool grey-brown dawn shadow, darker than daylight.

Simeon, a very old, thin, bent man with a long uneven white beard, in a plain worn patched grey-brown wool robe and a simple undyed head cloth, stands on the lowest step and holds the small swaddled baby in his trembling arms close to his chest, looking down at him, tears of joy running down his wrinkled cheeks, his lips parted in a quiet smile, mid-word. Around the baby's head there is only a very soft, faint warm glow, no bright disc, no rays.

Mary, a thin village girl of about fifteen, in sandals, in a plain faded blue-grey homespun dress with a sewn patch, a rough undyed head cloth, stands half a step forward toward Simeon, her hands half raised toward the child as if ready to take him back, a little worried, watching.

Joseph, a plain young carpenter with a short dark beard, in a worn brown wool tunic and patched cloak, stands a little behind Mary with a small clear gap of dark wall between Mary's hand and his cloak. He holds LOW at his hip, by its rim, a small crude hand-woven reed basket-cage, lumpy and uneven, with a flat woven lid tied down with twine; through the gaps of the weave two small grey turtledoves peek out. No dome, no ring, no metal. He glances at Simeon. Keep small dark gaps between all three figures.

The place: a plain, poor corner of a stone Temple courtyard: two thick rough uneven stone pillars, chipped and weathered, standing straight on the floor with no capitals and no bases, one of them partly cut off by the left edge of the frame; a plain old plastered stone wall with a dark doorway on the right; worn uneven steps; cracked uneven flagstones; a simple clay water basin. No gold, no carving, no decoration, no arches, no trees.

Light: one single narrow, low, warm morning sunbeam enters at a shallow angle from the left, about one sixth of the frame wide, and lands only on Simeon, the child and the step at his feet. Inside the beam a few dust motes and at most one or two faint spiral swirls. The upper left of the picture and the rest of the courtyard stay in deep cool shadow; the overall picture is low-key and dim.

Composition: candid and asymmetric; Simeon right of centre on the lowest step, Mary and Joseph left of centre. The middle foreground between Joseph and Simeon is an open empty patch of flagstone floor, and the lowest step in front of Simeon is a clear flat ledge lit by the beam.
