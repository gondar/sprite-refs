SCENE: The Presentation in the Temple: a poor young couple brings their forty-day-old baby to the Temple in Jerusalem, in the cold early morning just after sunrise.

Simeon, a very old, thin, bent man with a long uneven white beard, in a plain worn grey-brown wool robe and a simple undyed head cloth, holds the small swaddled baby in his arms close to his chest, looking down at him, tears of joy running down his wrinkled cheeks, his lips parted in a quiet smile, mid-word. The baby's face gives off a very soft warm glow, the only holy light.

Mary, a thin village girl of about fifteen, barefoot, in a plain faded blue-grey homespun dress with a sewn patch, a rough undyed head cloth, stands one step away from Simeon, her hands half raised toward the child as if ready to take him back, a little worried, watching.

Joseph, a plain young carpenter with a short dark beard, in a worn brown wool tunic and patched cloak, stands slightly behind Mary holding up a small cheap woven wicker cage; inside sit two small grey turtledoves, the poor family's offering, clearly visible. Keep small dark gaps between the three figures.

The place: a plain, simple corner of a stone Temple courtyard: worn pale limestone steps, two or three heavy undecorated stone columns, cracked uneven flagstones, a plain stone wall with a dark doorway, a simple clay water basin. Old, quiet, a little cold. No gold, no carving, no decoration, no arches.

Light: one low warm morning sunbeam slants in between two columns from the left and falls on Simeon and the child; everything else sits in cool grey-brown shadow.

Composition: candid and asymmetric; Simeon right of centre on the lowest step, Mary and Joseph left of centre. The lower-left foreground is an open empty patch of flagstone floor, and the bottom step on the right is a clear flat ledge.

Do not copy their content. Take the low, quiet key of Image 1 (deep shadow, one glowing light source), not the brightness of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, textured gouache fills with paper grain, slightly flattened perspective. Spirals live only in nature and light (tree trunks, olive leaves, wind, the rays of light), never as ornament on clothes or buildings. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads on long necks, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: LOW KEY, dim and earthy. Early dawn half-light: most of the picture sits in deep cool shadow (umber, dusty brown, slate blue, dark ochre, olive), with ONE warm light source making a single pool of soft amber light. Muted, desaturated, quiet colours like old clay and wool. Few flowers or none. No pastel, no mint green, no pink, no bright sky.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: rough uneven stone, cracked mud plaster, bare packed earth, coarse undyed wool, patched cloth, chipped clay pots. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders on clothing.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, slightly off-centre, as if the camera caught a passing moment; the figures are not posed for the viewer and do not look at the viewer; objects lie where they were dropped or used. Wide 16:9, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. Each figure is a readable silhouette against the background. Only the people described appear, and the two small turtledoves inside the wicker cage; no other animals or birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
