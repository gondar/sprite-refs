SCENE: The Nativity, in a poor rock cave used as a stable outside Bethlehem, in the cold first grey light before dawn, an hour after the birth.

Mary, a thin village girl of about fifteen, exhausted, lies half-propped on a heap of straw against the rock wall, a coarse undyed wool blanket over her legs, her plain faded blue-grey homespun dress patched, a rough head cloth slipping back from her damp hair. She holds the tiny newborn, tightly swaddled in plain cream cloth strips, up against her cheek, her eyes half closed, a tired tender smile, nuzzling him. The baby's small face gives off a very soft warm glow, the only holy light.

Joseph, a plain tired young carpenter with a short dark beard, in a worn brown wool tunic and patched cloak, kneels a little apart from her on the dirt floor, bending low over a tiny fire of sticks and blowing on it to make it catch, his hands cupped around it, glancing back over his shoulder at Mary and the child. There is a clear dark space between Joseph and Mary.

The place: a low rough cave of dark brown and grey rock with a sagging wooden beam and a rough rock ledge along the back wall; packed earth floor with loose straw scattered everywhere. In the middle ground stands a crude, empty wooden feeding trough for animals, roughly nailed boards on splayed legs, half full of straw, clearly visible. In the dim back of the cave a big patient ox lies in shadow, only its head lit faintly, a soft cloud of breath steaming from its nostrils in the cold. A cracked clay jar, a walking stick leaning on the rock, a rope hanging from the beam. At the left edge the cave mouth shows a thin cold slate-blue dawn sky with one fading star.

Light: the little fire is the single warm light, a small pool of amber on Mary, the baby and Joseph; everything else in deep cool brown and slate shadow.

Composition: candid and asymmetric; Mary and the baby right of centre, Joseph kneeling at the fire left of centre, the trough between and slightly behind them. The lower-left foreground is an open patch of bare earth and straw, empty, with a flat low rock beside it.
