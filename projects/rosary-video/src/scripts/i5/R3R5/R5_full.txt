SCENE: The Finding in the Temple: after three days of searching, Mary and Joseph find the twelve-year-old Jesus sitting among the teachers on the porch steps of the Temple in Jerusalem, in the early morning.

Jesus, a thin boy of twelve with dark hair, in a plain undyed homespun tunic, barefoot, sits on a worn stone step leaning forward eagerly, one finger on a line of an open scroll, asking a question, his face bright with interest.

Two old teachers sit with him on the steps, in plain worn grey and brown wool robes and simple head cloths, nothing rich: one holds a long unrolled parchment scroll across his knees, eyebrows raised in surprise; the other, with a long grey beard, strokes his beard and listens closely. Several more rolled scrolls lie on the step beside them.

Mary, a thin village girl, barefoot and dusty from three days on the road, in a plain faded blue-grey homespun dress with a patch and a rough head cloth, has just come round a column at the side in the foreground, one hand pressed to her chest, relieved, almost in tears; Joseph, a plain carpenter with a short dark beard in a worn dusty brown cloak, is right behind her with his hand on her shoulder, a travel bundle on his back. A clear gap of stone separates the parents from the group on the steps.

The place: a plain, simple Temple porch: wide worn pale limestone steps, two heavy undecorated stone columns, a plain stone wall, cracked flagstones, a clay oil lamp gone out on a step. No gold, no carving, no decoration, no arches.

Light: one low warm morning sunbeam slants in from the left under the porch onto Jesus and the open scroll; the rest sits in cool grey-brown dawn shadow.

Composition: candid and asymmetric; Jesus and the teachers on the steps right of centre, Mary and Joseph at the left side in the foreground. In the lower middle foreground the lowest step and the flagstones are open and empty.

Do not copy their content. Take the low, quiet key of Image 1 (deep shadow, one glowing light source), not the brightness of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, textured gouache fills with paper grain, slightly flattened perspective. Spirals live only in nature and light (tree trunks, olive leaves, wind, the rays of light), never as ornament on clothes or buildings. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads on long necks, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: LOW KEY, dim and earthy. Early dawn half-light: most of the picture sits in deep cool shadow (umber, dusty brown, slate blue, dark ochre, olive), with ONE warm light source making a single pool of soft amber light. Muted, desaturated, quiet colours like old clay and wool. Few flowers or none. No pastel, no mint green, no pink, no bright sky.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: rough uneven stone, cracked mud plaster, bare packed earth, coarse undyed wool, patched cloth, chipped clay pots. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders on clothing.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, slightly off-centre, as if the camera caught a passing moment; the figures are not posed for the viewer and do not look at the viewer; objects lie where they were dropped or used. Wide 16:9, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. Each figure is a readable silhouette against the background. Only the people described appear; no animals, no birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
