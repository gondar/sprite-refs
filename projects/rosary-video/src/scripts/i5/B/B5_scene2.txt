SCENE: The Crucifixion, shown symbolically from a distance: the bare hill of Golgotha at night.

On top of a bare rocky hill in the upper middle of the picture stands a single plain wooden cross, seen only as a dark silhouette against the sky, no body detail; the cross is about one seventh of the picture height, clearly readable, with a thin glowing crimson line of horizon light just behind it. It is the first thing the eye sees.
In the middle ground, right of centre, on the gentle slope at the foot of the hill, Mary and John stand on the ground, small in the landscape (each about two fifths of the picture height, their feet at about four fifths of the picture height). Mary, the young girl from Image 3, stands looking up toward the far cross, her hands folded at her chest, her face sad but calm. John, a young beardless disciple with dark hair in a plain brown tunic, stands half a step behind her at her side, one hand resting gently on her shoulder, also looking up at the cross. Their heads do NOT touch: a thin gap of night sky shows between their faces. No other people.

The place: a bare stony slope with dry grass and scattered stones, one bent gnarled olive tree with a spiral trunk on the left side. In the lower right foreground lies one large flat grey rock with an empty bare flat top, a little apart from the figures (open ground between the rock and them). In the lower left corner dark, plain rock and earth. The lower centre is open, empty stony ground. The sky is deep indigo night with big slow spiral clouds, calmer near the corners; the thin crimson line just above the horizon behind the hill is the only warm light, faintly rimming the cross and the two figures. Everything else is indigo, violet and slate-blue.

Composition: wide and quiet, lots of sky, the cross high in the middle on the line of Mary's gaze; Mary and John right of centre in the middle ground, small; the foreground open.
