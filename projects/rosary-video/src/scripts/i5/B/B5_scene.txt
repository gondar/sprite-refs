SCENE: The Crucifixion, shown symbolically from a distance: a bare hill of Golgotha at night.

On top of a bare rocky hill, far away in the upper middle of the picture, stands a single plain wooden cross seen only as a small dark silhouette against the sky; it is small and distant, no body detail visible.
In the middle ground, at the foot of the hill, a little right of centre, Mary and John stand together holding each other. Mary, a woman of about forty-five, in a worn blue-grey wool mantle over her head, rests her head on John's shoulder; John, a young beardless man with dark hair in a plain brown tunic, holds her gently with his arm around her shoulders; both look up toward the cross, faces sad but calm and peaceful. No other people.

The place: a bare stony hill with dry grass and scattered stones, one bent gnarled olive tree with a spiral trunk on the left side. The sky is deep indigo night with big slow spiral clouds; just above the horizon behind the hill runs ONE thin glowing crimson line of light, the only warm light in the picture, faintly rimming the cross and the two figures. Everything else is indigo, violet and slate-blue.

Composition: wide and quiet, lots of sky; the cross small and high in the middle; Mary and John right of centre in the middle ground; the lower left foreground is open empty stony ground, with a flat rock on the lower right.
