SCENE: The Crowning with Thorns, told symbolically and gently: a dim guardroom at night, a moment of quiet dignity.

Jesus, a thin man of about thirty with long dark hair and a short beard, sits on a rough wooden bench left of centre. Over his plain undyed tunic he wears an old faded violet-purple cloak, worn and threadbare. He holds a simple thin reed loosely across his knees with both hands, like a sceptre nobody asked for. A plain dark crown woven of thorny twigs rests lightly on his hair. His head is slightly bowed, his eyes half-closed, his face peaceful and patient. He is unhurt.

The soldiers are NOT in the picture: only their dark SHADOWS fall across the plaster wall behind him, cast by the lamp: one shadow of a man bowing mockingly, one shadow pointing; their owners are outside the frame.

The place: a poor guardroom with a cracked mud-plaster wall, a small high window with a slice of indigo night sky and the moon, straw scattered on a packed-earth floor, a clay jug, a small cup tipped over on the floor, a small crimson-red rag dropped on the end of the bench. One small clay oil lamp burns on a stone ledge on the right; it is the only warm light, making a soft pool around Jesus; everything else is deep indigo and violet shadow.

Composition: Jesus on the bench left of centre; the shadows on the wall behind and to the right; the lower part of the frame is open empty earth floor, and on the right a low wooden crate stands near the bench.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, textured gouache fills with paper grain, slightly flattened perspective. Spirals live only in nature and light (olive trees, clouds, wind, smoke, the rays of light), never as ornament on clothes or buildings. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads on long necks, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.

LIGHT AND PALETTE: NIGHT, LOW KEY. The whole picture sits in deep indigo, slate blue and violet shadow, with ONE light source making a single soft pool of light; quiet, desaturated, muted colours like old wool and stone. Exactly ONE small crimson accent in the whole image (a piece of cloth). No pastel, no bright colours, no gold.
