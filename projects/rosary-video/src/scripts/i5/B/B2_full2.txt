SCENE: The Scourging at the Pillar, told symbolically and gently: a cold, quiet stone courtyard in Jerusalem at night, the moment of waiting.

Jesus, a thin man of about thirty with long dark hair and a short beard, in a plain undyed homespun tunic, barefoot, stands beside a single rough unhewn stone pillar, a little left of centre (the pillar is one rough upright stone, no capital, no base, its top hidden beyond the upper edge of the picture). His wrists are tied together in front of him with a coarse rope whose end is looped loosely around the pillar. His head is bowed, eyes lowered, face calm and patient. His dark crimson-red cloak lies dropped in a heap on the flagstones at the foot of the pillar, on its left side. He is unhurt and fully clothed.

The soldiers are NOT in the picture: only two long blue-black SHADOWS of standing men fall across the plaster wall on the right, soft-edged, their owners outside the frame. No weapons, no whips.

The place: a poor walled courtyard of cracked plaster and rough stone, all deep blue-grey in moonlight, worn uneven flagstones. A low stone step runs along the right wall in the lower right; its top is empty. A wooden bucket stands at the near-left end of the step, nothing else on the step. One small torch burns in an iron ring on the left wall, its smoke curling in a spiral; it makes ONE small warm pool of light only on Jesus, the pillar and the floor right around them (about a quarter of the frame wide); the right wall and everything else stays cold indigo with no warm light. Above the wall a deep indigo night sky with a pale moon among spiral clouds; a thin cracked-wall vine creeps on the left wall.

Composition: pillar and Jesus left of centre; the right third is the plain cold wall with the two long shadows; the lower centre-right foreground is open empty flagstones.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, slightly flattened perspective, visible pencil strokes and gouache texture, not clean vector lines. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: small heads on long necks, almond eyes with dot pupils, tiny mouths, long slender hands, simple clothes drawn as plain flat shapes.
