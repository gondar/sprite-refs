SCENE: Jesus Carrying the Cross, on a narrow poor street of Jerusalem at late dusk, just before night.

Jesus, a thin man of about thirty with long dark hair and a short beard, in a plain undyed tunic with a dark crimson-red cloak, walks slowly from left to right, bent forward under a plain rough wooden cross whose crossbeam rests on his right shoulder; one hand steadies the beam. His face is tired but calm.
Behind him, a little gap away, Simon of Cyrene, a sturdy broad-shouldered peasant in a patched brown work tunic, lifts the lower end of the long upright beam with both arms, helping, his face kind and willing.
In front of them, at the side of the street, Veronica, a poor young woman in a grey-blue head cloth, kneels and holds out a plain white linen cloth toward Jesus with both hands.
There are no soldiers and no crowd in the picture.

The place: a narrow, poor street of uneven stones rising gently to the right between rough mud-brick and stone house walls, a closed wooden shutter, a broken clay jar by a doorstep, a low stone wall ledge on the lower right. Dust hangs in the air in soft spirals. At the far end of the street the last thin band of dull amber dusk glows low on the horizon under a deep indigo sky with spiral clouds; it is the only warm light, backlighting the figures; the rest is indigo and slate-blue shadow.

Composition: the three figures across the middle band, Jesus slightly right of centre, each a readable silhouette with small gaps between them; the lower left foreground is open empty cobbles.
