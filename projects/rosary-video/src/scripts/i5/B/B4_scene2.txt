SCENE: Jesus Carrying the Cross, on a narrow poor street of Jerusalem at late dusk, just before night.

Jesus, a thin man of about thirty with long dark hair and a short beard, in a plain undyed tunic with a dark crimson-red cloak, walks slowly from left to right, bent forward. A plain rough wooden cross lies ON HIM: the crossing point of the two beams rests ON his right shoulder, the crossbeam lies across his shoulder, and his right hand steadies it. The long upright beam trails down BEHIND him diagonally to the right toward the ground. His face is tired but calm.
Behind him, a little gap away, Simon of Cyrene, a lean weathered peasant labourer of normal build (no bigger than Jesus), in a patched brown work tunic, bends and lifts the FAR LOWER END of the long upright beam with both arms. His face is serious and kind, eyes on Jesus, lips closed; no grin, no smirk.
In front of them, on the left side of the street with a clear gap of street between her and Jesus, Veronica, a poor woman of about thirty in an undyed oatmeal head cloth and a faded brown dress, kneels and holds out a plain white linen cloth toward Jesus with both hands.
There are no soldiers and no crowd in the picture.

The place: a narrow, poor street of uneven stones rising gently to the right between rough mud-brick and stone house walls, a closed wooden shutter, a broken clay jar by a doorstep on the left. A low square stone block stands at the lower right with a flat empty top. Dust hangs in the air in soft spirals. At the far end of the street the last thin band of dull amber dusk glows low on the horizon under a deep indigo sky; the sky has soft, low-contrast spiral clouds. The dusk glow is the only warm light, backlighting the figures; the rest is indigo and slate-blue shadow.

Composition: Veronica left, Jesus in the centre, Simon behind him right of centre; the lower foreground cobbles under and around Simon's feet are open and empty; the stone block lower right is empty.
