CHARACTER DESIGN (most important): the people are drawn exactly like the characters of The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers, very stylised and graphic, NOT like a modern children's picture book. Heads are simple geometric shapes; faces are flat with no shading, two small almond-shaped eyes drawn as dark ink shapes, a tiny line or dot for the nose, a tiny mouth. Long slender necks. Bodies built from bold flat triangles and trapezoids: robes are single flat triangular shapes widening to the hem, with one or two decorative pattern bands; arms and hands are thin, elongated and expressive. Hair and beards are flat shapes with a few spiral curls. Strong clean silhouettes readable at a glance, confident dark pencil outlines, flat colour fills with a little paper texture.
