STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon's The Secret of Kells and Wolfwalkers, pushed harder toward bold graphic design: large flat areas of colour, strong readable silhouettes, confident dark pencil outlines, textured gouache fills with paper grain. Nature is stylised into Celtic spirals: trees, clouds, wind and light rays drawn as decorative swirls. Flattened, slightly isometric perspective like an illuminated manuscript. Figures are angular and stylised in the Kells manner: robes drawn as simple triangular shapes, long necks, small round heads, almond eyes, tiny mouths, long slender hands. Holiness is shown only with light and pattern, a soft circle of light, never gold plates.
MOOD: intimate, human, tender and a little playful, a captured moment in the middle of a gesture, like a frame from an animated film. Not solemn, not a church painting, no frozen ceremonial poses, not realistic, not 3D.

COMPOSITION RULES: wide 16:9, the painted world fills the canvas edge to edge. The figures stand clearly apart against the background, each figure a separate readable silhouette with a little background visible between separate figures. In the lower third a winding ground path crosses the foreground from left to right; its surface stays open and empty, with pebbles, grass tufts and flowers only along its edges. Only the people described appear; no animals, no birds.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
