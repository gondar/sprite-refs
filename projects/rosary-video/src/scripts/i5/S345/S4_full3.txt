Image 1, Image 2 and Image 3 are STYLE REFERENCES only. Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its harsh-noon earthy palette (dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached hazy sky), its grounded sober mood, and the simple natural way it draws people. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the Celtic spirals hidden in nature (clouds, wind and rays of light). Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the night palette of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Spirals appear only subtly in nature and light, never as ornament. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched undyed homespun clothes, bare feet, rough wood, chipped clay, frayed rope. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders. Holiness is shown only through light, never by halos.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, as if the camera caught a passing moment; nobody looks at the viewer; objects lie where they were used. Keep small gaps between the figures. Wide 16:9, full bleed.

Create a new wide 16:9 illustration: the Transfiguration of Jesus on the top of Mount Tabor at harsh midday.

SETTING: a bare rocky mountain top at noon: broken ochre and clay boulders with faint natural curving strata lines in the rock (not carved spirals, not symbols), dry khaki grass and a few thorn bushes; far below and behind, a hazy plain of olive and sand fields and distant hills; a warm dusty ochre hazy sky like Image 1 (no blue, no mint), slightly darker and heavier toward the top edge, with one low heavy cloud drifting just behind and to the right of Jesus; the upper-left sky stays calm and plain. On the rocks: a dropped wooden walking staff, a small cloth bundle and a pair of worn sandals.

THE LIGHT: Jesus himself is the single source of a blinding, pure white light. It radiates from him in broad soft rays whose edges curl into Celtic spirals across the sky and the rocks, bleaching the nearby stone pale and casting long shadows away from him. The light is white and silver, never gold. His plain clothes turn dazzling white in it. This light is the only magical element. The rocks away from Jesus stay in mid-tone ochre, clay and olive with deep blue-brown noon shadows; ONLY the area around Jesus is bleached white by his light. Overall the picture is mid-toned, not pale: the foreground rocks and the bottom of the frame are in fairly deep warm shadow so that Jesus's white light is clearly the brightest thing.

FIGURES (candid, mid-moment, no pathos):
- Jesus: an ordinary young working man of about thirty, dark hair and short beard, barefoot, standing upright on a flat rock slightly right of centre and a little higher than the others, face calm and gentle, eyes lowered toward his friends. He wears one plain ankle-length homespun tunic with a rope belt (no trousers); it glows white in the light. He stands slightly turned toward his friends, weight on one leg, one hand a little more open than the other. No halo ring, no crown.
- Three simple apostles in plain rough patched brown and grey homespun, barefoot. Three apostles spread out across the lower left in a loose diagonal, each one a separate silhouette with a clear strip of bare rock (at least a hand's width) between them; no body, limb or garment of one touches another: Peter, a stocky older man with grey curly beard, half-kneeling, one hand raised to shield his eyes, mouth open as if about to speak; James, a dark-bearded man, kneeling forward with his face hidden in his arm; John, a beardless youth, sitting back on the rocks, peeking through his fingers in wonder.
Figures are of moderate size; Jesus's head at about 25 percent of the frame height from the top.

COMPOSITION: wide shot, asymmetric: Jesus right of centre, the three apostles on the lower left in a loose diagonal: all three on the same ground level in one loose row from left to right, nobody behind or above another: John further left, Peter in the middle, James to the right of Peter, with a wide strip of bare rock between each pair. Keep the very bottom-left corner of the frame a flat, empty, table-like boulder with a level top. The bottom right corner is a calm flat boulder in sun, and the lower foreground has open flat rock surfaces. The only living beings are these four people.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, no halos, no gold, no angels, no extra figures, no sparkles, no flowers, no animals, no birds, full-bleed image.
