Image 1, Image 2 and Image 3 are STYLE REFERENCES only. Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its harsh-noon earthy palette (dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached hazy sky), its grounded sober mood, and the simple natural way it draws people. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the Celtic spirals hidden in nature (rock strata, water curls, clouds, rays of light). Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the night palette of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Spirals appear only subtly in nature and light, never as ornament. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched undyed homespun clothes, bare feet, rough wood, chipped clay, frayed rope. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders. Holiness is shown only through light, never by halos.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, as if the camera caught a passing moment; nobody looks at the viewer; objects lie where they were used. Keep small gaps between the figures. Wide 16:9, full bleed.

Create a new wide 16:9 illustration: Jesus teaching poor fishermen on the shore of the Sea of Galilee at harsh midday.

SETTING: a pebbly lakeshore at noon. The lake is calm olive-grey water with a few turquoise glints of sun and small spiral curls of ripples; across the water low dry ochre hills under a pale hazy sky with a few heavy clouds. On the shore: heaped brown fishing nets, two worn wicker baskets, a coil of rope, a clay water jar, flat stones and a piece of driftwood, left where they were used. Harsh sun from above with short deep blue-brown shadows. From a gap in the clouds a faint, soft column of sunlight with curling spiral edges falls on Jesus; this light is the only magical element.

FIGURES (candid, mid-moment, not posing):
- Jesus: an ordinary young working man of about thirty, dark hair and short beard, plain worn undyed brown-beige tunic, barefoot. He sits sideways on the edge of an old patched wooden fishing boat that is pulled up half onto the shore, slightly left of centre, leaning a little forward and speaking simply, one hand raised and open, palm up, as if explaining something. Friendly, plain, no halo.
- A weathered middle-aged fisherman, sun-burnt, in a faded patched tunic with sleeves rolled up, sits on a stone to the right with a net across his knees; he has stopped mending it, the wooden needle still in his hand, and looks up at Jesus, thoughtful. A clear gap of shore between him and Jesus.
- A small barefoot child of about six, in a short rough tunic, sits on the pebbles between them but a little nearer the viewer, hugging her knees, listening with wide eyes.
- Further back, a younger thin fisherman stands knee-deep in the water by a second boat, holding a rope, half-turned to listen.
Figures are of moderate size, Jesus's head at about 25 percent of the frame height.

COMPOSITION: wide shot from the beach. Sky and hills fill the top quarter, the lake the middle band, the boat and figures left-of-centre to right. The bottom quarter is an open stretch of pale pebbles and flat stones with an overturned basket at the far lower left; the lower left and lower right corners stay calm and open. The only living beings are these four people.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, no halos, no sparkles, no flowers, no animals, no birds, no fish visible, full-bleed image.
