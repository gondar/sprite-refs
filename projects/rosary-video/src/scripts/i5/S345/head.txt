Image 1, Image 2 and Image 3 are STYLE REFERENCES only. Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its harsh-noon earthy palette (dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached hazy sky), its grounded sober mood, and the simple natural way it draws people. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the Celtic spirals hidden in nature (rock strata, water curls, clouds, rays of light). Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the night palette of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Spirals appear only subtly in nature and light, never as ornament. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched undyed homespun clothes, bare feet, rough wood, chipped clay, frayed rope. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders. Holiness is shown only through light, never by halos.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, as if the camera caught a passing moment; nobody looks at the viewer; objects lie where they were used. Keep small gaps between the figures. Wide 16:9, full bleed.
