Image 1, Image 2 and Image 3 are STYLE REFERENCES only. Image 1 is the main reference for this part of the film: match its harsh-noon earthy palette (dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached hazy sky), its grounded sober mood, and the simple natural way it draws people. From all three take the hand-drawn pencil-and-gouache technique, paper grain, confident dark pencil outlines and the Celtic spirals hidden in nature (rock strata, water curls, clouds, rays of light). Do not copy their content or composition, and do not take the night palette of Image 2.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold simplified flat graphic shapes, confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Spirals appear only subtly in nature and light, never as ornament. People are drawn simply with natural proportions and quiet faces, like the people in Image 1.

POVERTY AND SIMPLICITY: everything is poor, worn and handmade: patched undyed homespun clothes, bare feet, rough wood, chipped clay, frayed rope. Nothing ornamental, nothing gold, no jewellery, no patterned borders. Holiness is shown only through light, never by halos.

COMPOSITION: candid and asymmetric, as if the camera caught a passing moment; nobody looks at the viewer; objects lie where they were used. Keep small gaps between the figures. Wide 16:9, full bleed.

Create a new wide 16:9 illustration: the Transfiguration of Jesus on the top of Mount Tabor at harsh midday.

SETTING: a bare rocky mountain top at noon: broken ochre and clay boulders with spiral strata lines, dry khaki grass and a few thorn bushes; far below and behind, a hazy plain of olive and sand fields and distant hills; a pale hazy sky with one low heavy cloud drifting just behind the summit. On the rocks: a dropped wooden walking staff, a small cloth bundle and a pair of worn sandals.

THE LIGHT: Jesus himself is the single source of a blinding, pure white light. It radiates from him in broad soft rays whose edges curl into Celtic spirals across the sky and the rocks, bleaching the nearby stone pale and casting long shadows away from him. The light is white and silver, never gold. His plain clothes turn dazzling white in it. This light is the only magical element, and the rest of the picture stays in the earthy noon palette.

FIGURES (candid, mid-moment, no pathos):
- Jesus: an ordinary young working man of about thirty, dark hair and short beard, barefoot, standing upright on a flat rock slightly right of centre and a little higher than the others, arms relaxed and slightly open at his sides, face calm and gentle, eyes lowered toward his friends. His simple tunic glows white. No halo ring, no crown.
- Three simple apostles in plain rough patched brown and grey homespun, barefoot, fallen on the rocks in the lower left, with clear gaps between them: Peter, a stocky older man with grey curly beard, half-kneeling, one hand raised to shield his eyes, mouth open as if about to speak; James, a dark-bearded man, kneeling forward with his face hidden in his arm; John, a beardless youth, sitting back on the rocks, peeking through his fingers in wonder.
Figures are of moderate size; Jesus's head at about 25 percent of the frame height from the top.

COMPOSITION: wide shot, asymmetric: Jesus right of centre, the three apostles on the lower left in a loose diagonal. The bottom right corner is a calm flat boulder in sun, and the lower foreground has open flat rock surfaces. The only living beings are these four people.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, no halos, no gold, no angels, no extra figures, no sparkles, no flowers, no animals, no birds, full-bleed image.
