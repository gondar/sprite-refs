CHARACTER DESIGN (most important): draw the people the way Tomm Moore draws Brendan, Aidan and Aisling in The Secret of Kells and Robyn and Mebh in Wolfwalkers — very stylised, graphic and angular, like moving illuminated-manuscript figures. Each head is a simple geometric shape (an oval or a rounded square) on a long thin neck. Faces are flat pale shapes with no shading and no modelling: two small almond-shaped ink eyes, a tiny nose line, a tiny curved mouth, rosy cheek dots. Bodies are built from bold flat geometric pieces: a robe is one tall flat triangle widening to the hem, decorated with a single band of Celtic pattern; arms and fingers are thin, long and expressive; feet are small. Hair and beards are flat graphic shapes ending in spiral curls. Proportions are elongated and elegant, never chubby, never realistic. Strong clean silhouettes, confident dark pencil outlines, flat colour fills with paper texture.
