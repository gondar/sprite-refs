SCENE: The Visitation as a joyful, windy reunion on a hilltop on a spring morning. Palette: pale mint green, cream, blush rose, soft pale gold light, white flowers; fresh, airy, luminous.

A grassy hilltop with a little whitewashed stone house with a round door on the right, stylised spiral trees bending in the wind, wind drawn as curling Celtic spiral lines, and a big pale golden sun disc low in the sky behind the two women, between them and slightly above.
Left of centre, young Mary, a girl of about fifteen in a blush-rose dress and pale mint veil, has just run up the hill: she leans forward, out of breath and happy, one foot still behind her, her veil and robe streaming back in the wind, reaching both hands forward.
Right of centre, her older cousin Elizabeth, a warm elderly woman in a cream and sage-green robe with a soft white veil, LAUGHS WITH JOY, head thrown slightly back, reaching both hands to take Mary's hands. Their hands just meet in the middle; apart from the joined hands, their bodies stand clearly apart with sky and sun visible between them. Robes and veils blow in the wind, playful, tender, mid-motion.
The figures stand on the hilltop in the middle band of the picture; below them in the lower third an open winding path crosses the foreground.
