Create a new wide 16:9 illustration: the Baptism of Jesus in the river Jordan, a poor, plain, real place at harsh midday.

STYLE: a flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells), but grounded, sober and earthy, like a frame from a serious animated film for children rather than a storybook. Bold, simplified, flat graphic shapes: hills drawn as big flat layered ochre and clay shapes with spiral strata lines, rocks as simple flat slabs, reeds as rhythmic flat strokes; confident dark pencil outlines, dry gouache with visible paper grain. Spirals appear only subtly: in the grain of rocks, the curl of the river current, the edges of clouds and the rays of light. Muted earthy palette: dusty ochre, khaki, olive, clay, sand, bleached pale sky; a little turquoise only where sunlight glints on the water. Harsh noon light from above with short deep blue-brown shadows.

SETTING: the Jordan is a wide, slow, muddy olive-brown desert river. Behind it rise dry, rocky, bare hills of ochre and clay with a few scrubby thorn bushes; tall tough reeds and rushes line both banks. Sky pale and hazy with heat, with a few heavy clouds; in one place the clouds part and a single soft column of light falls straight down exactly onto Jesus's head and shoulders, its edges drawn as faint curling spiral rays — this light is the only magical element in the picture. A small, simple, realistic white-grey dove, wings open, flies down inside the column of light, high above Jesus's head in open sky, well separated from everyone. Far in the background on the opposite bank, a few small, simple people in plain dusty clothes wait in a loose irregular line, tiny and unimportant, partly hidden by reeds.

FIGURES (candid, caught in the middle of the moment, not posing for the viewer):
- Jesus: an ordinary young working man of about thirty, dark hair and a short beard, in a plain worn undyed brown-beige tunic, standing waist-deep in the muddy river, slightly left of centre, head bowed a little, eyes closed, humble and quiet, arms relaxed. No halo, no glow on him except the light falling from above.
- John the Baptist: to the right of Jesus, also waist-deep, with a clear gap of water (about half a body width) between them. Gaunt, sun-burnt, thin and wiry, a wild unkempt dark beard and long matted hair, wearing a rough shaggy camel-hair garment tied with a plain leather belt. He leans toward Jesus and pours water from his two cupped hands, held just above Jesus's head, so a short thin stream of water falls onto Jesus's hair. His face is intense and joyful, eyes shining, a real man, not a caricature.
Both figures are of moderate size, their heads at about 30 percent of the frame height, drawn simply with natural proportions like the people in the style references.

COMPOSITION: wide shot from the near bank, slightly asymmetric. The hills and sky fill the top third. The river is wide and fills the middle band of the frame from about 38 to 70 percent of the height across the full width, with long open stretches of slow water on both sides of the two men. The bottom 30 percent is the near bank: an open stretch of cracked sandy mud and flat stones, with clumps of reeds only at the far left and right edges; the middle of the bank is empty. The only living beings are Jesus, John, the dove and the tiny distant people.
