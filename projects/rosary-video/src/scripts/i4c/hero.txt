A flat 2D hand-drawn animation key frame in the visual language of Cartoon Saloon and Tomm Moore's films (The Secret of Kells, Song of the Sea, Wolfwalkers).

Subject: a tiny hazel dormouse, the hero of a children's film. Compact round body built from simple circles and teardrop shapes: golden-ginger fur on the back and head, a soft cream belly and chin, huge round glossy dark eyes with a tiny white highlight, small round ears set on top of the head, a tiny pink nose, thin little paws. Its tail is big, thick and fluffy, ginger, drawn as one single flowing spiral shape that curls up behind it like a Celtic swirl — almost squirrel-like in volume, but the animal clearly reads as a small round dormouse, not a squirrel.

Action: the dormouse is mid-leap from left to right, body stretched in a joyful arc, front paws reaching forward toward a small glowing golden bead of light floating just ahead of it on the right. Eyes wide with wonder and delight. Very expressive, dynamic pose.

Setting: an abstract deep dark indigo space, filled with flowing golden Celtic spiral light trails, triskele curls and tiny star-like specks, like an illuminated manuscript page come alive at night. The golden bead casts a warm glow onto the dormouse's face and paws.

Composition: wide 16:9 frame, dormouse slightly left of centre, bead right of centre, plenty of breathing space.

Style: bold simple flat shapes, visible soft pencil outline in dark brown, textured gouache fill with paper grain, minimal facial features, limited palette of indigo, gold, ginger and cream, graphic and decorative like Pangur Bán the cat in The Secret of Kells.
