A background painting in the style of Arthur Rackham: pen-and-ink line with muted watercolor washes in sepia, olive, ochre and dusty rose on warm aged paper.
Scene: a tall, dense hazel hedge in a misty, overgrown old rose garden at dawn. Set into the middle of the hedge, like a small paper toy theatre (a cut-paper puppet stage) framed by arching rose vines with pale roses, is a very simplified, quiet depiction of the Annunciation: on the left a slender angel shown as a pale silhouette with tall folded wings holding a single white lily stem, on the right Mary kneeling in a simple blue-grey robe with her head bowed. The figures are flat and cut-paper-like, small and gentle, glowing softly in the mist.
In the foreground, on a mossy garden path, a tiny European hazel dormouse with golden-ginger fur, large dark eyes and a long furry tail runs from left to right, chasing a brown hazelnut that rolls ahead of it.
Wide shot, the paper theatre in the center-background, the dormouse small in the lower foreground. Calm, reverent, mysterious fairy-tale mood, soft diffused morning light. No text, no lettering, no watermark. 16:9 landscape.
