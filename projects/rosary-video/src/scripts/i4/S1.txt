SCENE (Luminous mystery, bright summer noon): The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan river. The river is drawn as stylised Celtic wave spirals in turquoise and teal. Jesus, a calm young man with simple long hair in a plain white cloth, stands waist-deep in the river with hands together. John the Baptist, a lean wild-haired man in a rough brown garment, stands on a rock at the bank pouring water over Jesus' head from a shell. Above, a white dove descends inside a column of bright warm-gold light with spiral rays coming down from a round sun opening in the sky. Emerald reeds and spiral trees on the banks. Palette: turquoise, teal, emerald, bright warm gold sun. The figures are in the middle band; the lower third is a flat sandy riverbank strip running across the foreground.

STYLE: A flat 2D hand-drawn animation background in the visual language of the Irish studio Cartoon Saloon (The Secret of Kells, Song of the Sea, Wolfwalkers). Bold flat shapes and strong graphic silhouettes. Nature stylised into Celtic spirals and knotwork: trees, waves, clouds, wind and light rays drawn as decorative swirls. Circular and geometric framing devices. Visible soft pencil linework, textured gouache and watercolour fills with paper grain. Flattened, slightly isometric perspective like an illuminated manuscript. Figures have simple stylised shapes: round heads, long elegant bodies, small almond eyes, minimal features, flowing robes drawn as single shapes. Holiness is shown with light and pattern — a soft circle of light, radiating spiral rays — never with heavy gold plates. Poetic, magical, a little mysterious and warm; not a church painting, not realistic, not 3D.

COMPOSITION: wide 16:9 establishing shot; the painted world fills the whole canvas edge to edge, with bold saturated shapes and confident dark pencil outlines. In the lower third, a winding ground path belonging naturally to the scene (drawn with the same texture, small pebbles, stylised grass tufts and flowers only along its edges) crosses the foreground from left to right; the path surface itself stays open, readable and free of figures and objects. Only the people described appear; the scene contains no animals and no birds other than any dove explicitly described.

Hard exclusions: no text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame, no border, full-bleed image.
