A character turnaround model sheet of a European hazel dormouse, drawn in the style of Arthur Rackham: fine pen-and-ink line with muted watercolor washes in sepia, ginger-ochre, olive and dusty rose, on a plain warm cream paper background with nothing else on it.
Three poses of the same dormouse, evenly spaced in a row from left to right, each clearly separate:
1. Side view, running fast along the ground in a bounding leap, body stretched, long furred tail streaming behind.
2. Front view, sitting upright on its haunches, holding a hazelnut in both tiny front paws against its chest, big dark eyes looking at the viewer.
3. Curled up asleep in a tight round ball, eyes closed, its long furry tail wrapped around its body and over its nose.
Consistent design in every pose: golden-ginger fur, pale cream throat and belly, very large glossy black eyes, small rounded ears, long fine whiskers, four limbs with tiny pink grasping fingers, a long evenly furred ginger tail about as long as the body. Delicate naturalistic Edwardian storybook drawing, not a cartoon. No text, no labels, no numbers, no watermark. 16:9 landscape.
