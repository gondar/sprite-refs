# R5 — Odnalezienie Pana Jezusa w świątyni (Radosne 5)
- **Event:** after three days of searching, Mary and Joseph find 12-year-old Jesus in the Temple among the teachers.
- **Jesus** (12, plain undyed tunic, bare feet): sits on a stone step, leaning forward eagerly, one hand pointing to a line on an open scroll, mid-question.
- **Two old teachers** (plain grey/brown wool, not rich): one holds the unrolled scroll on his knees, surprised, eyebrows up; the other strokes his beard, listening.
- **Mary and Joseph** (side foreground, dusty from the road): just arriving, Mary with a hand pressed to her chest, relieved; Joseph's hand on her shoulder. Small gap between the two groups.
- **Time/palette:** early morning, dim earthy dawn; UI #cfe6c4.
- **Props:** worn plain limestone porch steps, two heavy undecorated columns, a few scrolls lying on the step, a clay oil lamp gone out, Mary's travel bundle.
- **Single light:** low dawn sun from the left slanting under the porch onto Jesus and the scrolls.
- **Dormouse:** starts on the open lower step / flagstones foreground centre-left; one jump up to a higher step beside the lying scrolls; watches Jesus.
- **Caption:** top-left or top-right on plain stone wall above (calm area).
- **Memory symbol:** zwoje (unrolled scrolls).
