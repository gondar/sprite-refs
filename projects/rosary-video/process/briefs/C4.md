# C4 Wniebowzięcie Maryi — brief
- Event: Mary is taken up body and soul to heaven; her tomb is found empty and full of roses.
- Figures: Mary (simple, smiling, hands slightly open, gently rising in soft white light above the tomb); old apostle stopping with hands lifted in amazement; young apostle kneeling by the roses, holding one, looking up gladly. Gaps between them.
- Time/palette: golden dawn on cool blue-grey; sun rising behind hills on the right.
- Poor props: rough rock-cut tomb, folded linen cloth, dusty path, grey stones, olive trees with spiral trunks.
- Single light: the soft white glow around Mary (sun as distant secondary warmth).
- Dormouse: starts on the open dusty path lower-centre on the flat stone (idle_look); one jump (~3.5 s) to the rose-covered tomb lip / ground beside the roses; watches facing up toward Mary.
- Caption: bottom-right or top-left calm area.
- Memory symbol: GRÓB PEŁEN RÓŻ — the open empty tomb overflowing with roses.
