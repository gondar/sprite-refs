# C2 Wniebowstąpienie — brief
- Event: Jesus is taken up into heaven from a hilltop; the apostles watch him go.
- Figures (4 apostles, poor fishermen, small gaps): young one shades his eyes; old bearded one, mouth open, leans on staff; one half-kneels; one lifts a hand as if waving goodbye. Of Jesus only the white hem and two bare feet vanishing into the cloud.
- Time/palette: golden dawn on cool blue-grey (C1 look), peach/apricot glow, sage grass, small wild flowers.
- Poor props: worn footpath, grey stones, staffs, rough patched homespun, bare feet.
- Single light: the luminous cloud at top centre-left (white spiral rays).
- Dormouse: starts on the flat grey stone in the empty lower-left grassy slope (groom_wonder); one jump (~3.5 s) up the slope to a grass tuft left of the apostles' feet; watches facing right/up toward the cloud.
- Caption: bottom-left or top-left calm sky/grass (decide after layers; not under the cloud).
- Memory symbol: OBŁOK — the glowing cloud swallowing the hem and bare feet.
