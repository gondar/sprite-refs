# S1 — Chrzest w Jordanie (Światła 1, n=5)
- **Event:** John pours Jordan water over Jesus's bowed head; a dove of light comes down in a light column.
- **Gestures:** Jesus (waist-deep, head bowed): breath, bow, slight lift, slow sway with the water; overlays: tiny peaceful smile, eyes half-open to the water. John (grinning, arms out pouring): blink, joyful nod, laugh (eyes squeeze + head bobs, overlay wider laugh), breath, lean-in. Dove: flutter (wing beats ±6°, bob), glide, bob, single beat — wings only, the body stays a dove.
- **Palette/time:** Luminous = harsh noon, ochre and olive; light column from the cloud is the single holy light.
- **Props:** muddy pool, cattails, cracked shore slabs, three tiny onlookers far away.
- **Memory symbol:** water poured from cupped hands + the dove in the light column.
- **Dormouse:** at t=0 on the left shore slab (0.165, 0.89), grooming; at 3.6 s one jump to the shore edge left of Jesus (0.375, 0.88), watches facing right/up toward the dove and the pouring; last point 8.6.
- **Caption:** top-left (sky; the light column is centred).
