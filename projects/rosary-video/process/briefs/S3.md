# S3 — Głoszenie Królestwa i wzywanie do nawrócenia (Luminous 3)
- Event: Jesus teaches poor fishermen from a worn boat pulled up to the Galilee shore.
- Figures: Jesus sits on the boat's edge, speaking with one open hand, leaning slightly forward; a weathered fisherman pauses mid-net-mending, looking up; a small barefoot child sits on the pebbles, chin on knees, listening. (A second fisherman far back, optional.)
- Time/palette: harsh noon, ochre/olive, bleached hazy sky, short blue-brown shadows; turquoise glints only on water (as S1).
- Poor props: patched wooden boat, heaped nets, wicker baskets, a coil of rope, a clay jar, flat stones, beached driftwood.
- Light: sun from above; a faint soft column of light on Jesus only (spiral-ray edges), no halo.
- Dormouse: starts on a flat stone / overturned basket in the lower-left foreground; jumps once (~3.5 s) to the bow of the boat or a flat rock lower-right; watches Jesus, facing left.
- Caption: bottom-left over the empty pebble beach, or top-right on the plain sky.
- Memory symbol: ŁÓDŹ — the boat as a pulpit.
