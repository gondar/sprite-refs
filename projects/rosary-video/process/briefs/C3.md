# C3 Zesłanie Ducha Świętego — brief
- Event: the Holy Spirit comes on Mary and the apostles in the upper room, as wind and tongues of fire.
- Figures (Mary + 3 apostles, small gaps): Mary seated on a bench, hands open on her lap, quiet joyful smile looking up; old apostle half-opens his arms in astonishment; young one laughs, holding his head cloth in the wind; one kneels on the mat, eyes wide.
- Time/palette: golden dawn on cool blue-grey; dawn peach through the window, room corners blue-grey.
- Poor props: mud-plaster walls, low beams, bare floor, worn mat, clay jar, rough bench, plain linen curtain, loose parchment sheets.
- Single light: the small soft tongues of flame above each head (with dawn glow at the window as rim).
- Dormouse: starts on the open floor lower-right by the clay jar (stop_sniff); one jump (~3.5 s) onto the bench end / floor nearer the group (not over a face); watches facing left toward Mary.
- Caption: bottom-right or top-right over the plaster wall.
- Memory symbol: PŁOMYKI — one small flame over every head, plus the blowing curtain and flying papers.
