# R3 — Narodzenie Pana Jezusa (Radosne 3)
- **Event:** Jesus is born in a poor cave-stable outside Bethlehem; the first hour after the birth.
- **Mary** (~15, exhausted, tender): lies half-propped on straw, holding the swaddled newborn against her cheek, eyes half-closed, a tired smile. Mid-gesture: nuzzling the baby.
- **Joseph** (tired, plain): kneels by a tiny fire of sticks, blowing on it / warming his hands, glancing back at them. Gap between him and Mary.
- **Ox:** a dim shape in the back of the cave, breath steaming in the cold air (the only animal).
- **Time/palette:** first dawn, dim earthy (umber, slate, olive, dark ochre); UI #cfe6c4.
- **Props:** rough rock walls, dirt floor, scattered straw, a crude wooden feeding trough (żłóbek) with straw, a cracked clay jar, a coarse wool blanket, a walking stick leaning.
- **Single light:** the small fire's amber glow on Mary, the baby and Joseph; a thin cold grey dawn at the cave mouth. Holiness = a soft glow on the baby only.
- **Dormouse:** starts on the open straw/stone floor in the lower-left foreground; one jump up onto the rim of the empty feeding trough / a flat rock ledge; watches from there, facing Mary and the child.
- **Caption:** bottom-right or top-left on dark rock (calm area).
- **Memory symbol:** the żłóbek (wooden feeding trough with straw).
