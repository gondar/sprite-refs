# S4 — Przemienienie na górze Tabor (Luminous 4)
- Event: on a bare mountain top Jesus shines with blinding white light before three apostles.
- Figures: Jesus stands upright on a rock, arms slightly open at his sides, face calm, his plain clothes turned white by the light; Peter half-kneels, one hand shielding his eyes, mouth open as if about to speak; James and John fallen on the rocks, one hiding his face in his arm, the other peeking through his fingers.
- Time/palette: harsh noon, ochre/olive rock and dry grass, hazy pale sky; the white light is the only brightness. No gold.
- Poor props: rough boulders, thorn scrub, a dropped walking staff, a cloth bundle, sandals.
- Light: Jesus himself as the single source: white light radiating in Celtic spiral rays; a low cloud drifting behind.
- Dormouse: starts on a flat boulder at the lower-left foreground; jumps once to a rock ledge lower-right (clear of the apostles' faces); watches Jesus, facing up-left.
- Caption: bottom-right on bare rock or top-left on sky.
- Memory symbol: LŚNIĄCA GÓRA — a shining mountain top.
