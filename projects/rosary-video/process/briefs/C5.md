# C5 Ukoronowanie Maryi — brief
- Event: Mary is crowned Queen of heaven and earth — shown only as a circlet of twelve stars of light.
- Figure: Mary alone, humble young woman in a plain blue mantle on a soft cloud, hands loosely folded, quiet surprised smile looking slightly down; the circlet of 12 small white light-stars above her head (light, not metal).
- Time/palette: vast golden-dawn sky, peach/apricot low, cool blue-grey high; tiny misty hills of earth far below.
- Poor props: none needed — plain mantle, head cloth, bare feet; no throne, no angels, no gold.
- Single light: the ring of star-light and soft glow behind Mary.
- Dormouse: starts on the long flat cloud ledge lower-left (groom_wonder); one jump (~3.5 s) along the ledge toward Mary's cloud; watches facing right/up at the stars.
- Caption: top-left sky (calm) or bottom-right.
- Memory symbol: KORONA Z GWIAZD — twelve stars of light in a ring.
