# B2 Biczowanie (Bolesne 2) — v2 (owner change)
- Event: Jesus bound at the pillar in Pilate's courtyard; quiet waiting, nothing of the scourging shown.
- Figure: Jesus, half naked (bare torso, simple undyed cloth around the hips), barefoot, stands FACING the single rough stone pillar, chest to it, wrists tied with a coarse rope around the pillar. Three-quarter back view to the viewer, head in profile, slightly bowed, eye lowered, calm. Gesture: slow breath, the head bows and lifts a little.
- No blood, no wounds, no strike. Symbol on the floor: a coiled rope / a short whip lying still on the flagstones. Soldiers only as two still shadows on the right wall.
- Time/palette: night, indigo and slate, one crimson accent = the dropped cloak.
- Props: cracked plaster wall, worn flagstones, rope coil, wooden bucket, stone step.
- Light: one torch on the left wall; ONE soft warm circle of light around Jesus and the pillar; walls cold indigo.
- Dormouse: starts on the flagstones right of Jesus; one jump onto the stone step; watches the pillar facing left.
- Caption: top-right over the dark wall/sky.
- Symbol: the column with the rope (kolumna).
