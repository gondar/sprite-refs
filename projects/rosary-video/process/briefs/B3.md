# B3 Cierniem ukoronowanie (Bolesne 3)
- Event: in the guardroom Jesus sits mocked as a "king"; we show his quiet dignity, the mockery only as shadows.
- Figures: Jesus seated on a rough wooden bench, a faded worn purple cloak over his plain tunic, a simple reed held loosely in his hands across his knees, a plain dark crown of thorny twigs resting on his hair, head slightly bowed, eyes half-closed, peaceful. Mocking soldiers ONLY as shadows on the plaster wall (a bowing shadow, a pointing shadow).
- Time/palette: night, indigo and violet; the cloak is faded violet-purple; the single crimson accent = a small red rag dropped on the bench.
- Props: mud-plaster wall, a small high window with moon, a clay jug, a dice cup knocked over, straw on a packed-earth floor.
- Light: one oil lamp on a ledge to the right; the shadows on the wall come from it.
- Dormouse: starts on the earth floor (0.30,0.93); one jump up onto the left end of the bench (0.36,0.655); watches Jesus facing right.
- Caption: top-left over the dark wall.
- Symbol: korona cierniowa.
