# B4 Droga krzyżowa (Bolesne 4)
- Event: Jesus carries the cross up a narrow street toward Golgotha; helped and comforted.
- Figures: Jesus walking bent under a plain rough wooden cross resting on his right shoulder, one hand steadying it; Simon of Cyrene (lean labourer, patched brown tunic, serious and kind) lifting the far end of the upright; Veronica (about 30, oatmeal head cloth, brown dress) kneeling at the side of the street, holding out a plain white cloth toward him. Small gaps between them.
- Time/palette: late dusk turning to night: indigo sky with a last thin band of dull warm light low on the horizon; crimson accent = Jesus's red cloak.
- Props: narrow uneven stone street rising to the right, mud-brick walls, a closed shutter, a broken clay jar, dust in the air.
- Light: the low dusk glow at the end of the street, behind the figures.
- Dormouse: starts on the cobbles behind Simon (0.70,0.96); one jump onto the stone block (0.87,0.785); watches facing left.
- Caption: top-right over the dark wall.
- Symbol: krzyż na ramieniu.
