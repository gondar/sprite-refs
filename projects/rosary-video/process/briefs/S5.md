# S5 — Ustanowienie Eucharystii (Luminous 5), v3 (owner change: 13 people)
- Event: the Last Supper as a real poor meal: Jesus and EXACTLY 12 apostles sit on the floor around a low table/mat; Jesus breaks the bread, the clay cup stands by him.
- Figures (13, grouped for layers): Jesus behind the table facing us, mid-gesture breaking a flat loaf, eyes on the bread; back group: 2 apostles beside him (one young John leaning toward him); left group: 3 in profile; right group: 3 in profile; foreground: 4 with their backs to us. Candid, asymmetric, small gaps between groups.
- Time/palette: evening meal, Luminous harsh-ochre/olive family, low key, warm lamp light, deep umber shadow.
- Poor props: cracked mud plaster, beams, packed earth floor, reed mats, low rough plank table, flat loaves, one plain clay cup, chipped jug, clay bowls, oil lamp.
- Light: one oil lamp on the table near the bread and cup (single warm source); faint soft warmth on the bread only.
- Dormouse: starts on the open floor lower-left; one jump (~3.6 s) to the floor/mat edge lower-right of centre, clear of faces, bread and cup; watches facing the table.
- Caption: top-left or top-right on dark plaster/beams.
- Memory symbol: CHLEB I KIELICH — broken bread in Jesus's hands, clay cup beside him.
