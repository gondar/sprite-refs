# B5 Ukrzyżowanie (Bolesne 5)
- Event: Jesus dies on the cross on Golgotha; Mary and John stand beneath. Shown symbolically from a distance.
- Figures: the cross on the hilltop as a dark silhouette in the distance (12–15% of frame height, no body detail). In the middle ground (figures 40–45% of frame height, x 0.55–0.72) Mary — the SAME young village girl as R1 (round face, big dark eyes, beige rough head cloth, grey-blue patched dress, barefoot) — stands looking up at the cross with hands folded at her chest; John, a young beardless disciple in a plain brown tunic, half a step behind her, one hand on her shoulder; a gap of sky between their heads. Quiet, calm.
- Time/palette: night, deep indigo sky with spiral clouds; one thin crimson line on the horizon behind the hill is the crimson accent.
- Props: bare rocky hill, dry grass, a few stones, one bent olive tree at the side.
- Light: the thin crimson-amber horizon glow behind the cross (back light); faint moon-blue on the figures.
- Dormouse: starts on the right end of the flat rock (0.935,0.875); one jump up-left to (0.85,0.80), clear of John; watches the cross and Mary, facing left.
- Caption: bottom-left over dark calm rock and ground (0–0.30, 0.83–1.0).
- Symbol: Maryja pod krzyżem.
